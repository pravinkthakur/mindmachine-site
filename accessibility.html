Accessibility statement

Last updated 17 November 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who rely on assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done

  • Semantic HTML structure, with headings and landmarks that screen readers can navigate.
  • Full keyboard navigation, a skip-to-content link and visible focus states.
  • Text colour contrast of at least 4.5:1 against its background.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images and diagrams.
  • Layouts that adapt to small screens and to text zoom up to 200%.
  • Reduced animation for visitors who have asked their device for less motion.
  • Form fields with visible labels and clear error messages.

Known limitations

This website has not yet been independently audited. We test with automated tools and by hand, and we will fix any problems we find or are told about.

Feedback

If you find something hard to use, or need information in a different format, email david.higgins@mindmachine.io. We aim to reply within five working days.
