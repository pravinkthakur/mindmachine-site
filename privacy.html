Privacy notice

Last updated 24 November 2026

We collect as little personal data as we need, use it only to work with you, and keep it secure.

Who we are

This website is operated by Mobile Lab Ltd, trading as MindMachine (“MindMachine”, “we”, “us”). Registered in England and Wales, company number 08770672. Registered office: 158 High Street, Herne Bay, Kent, CT6 5NP. We are the controller of the personal data described in this notice, and are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office under registration number ZC287025.

Questions about this notice or your data: david.higgins@mindmachine.io.

What we collect

  • Enquiries. When you use our contact form or email us: your name, work email, organisation, role, and anything you choose to tell us about your problem.
  • Business relationships. If we work together: contact details of the people we work with, and records of our correspondence and agreements.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider records standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.

Please do not send us special category data (for example health information) or confidential client data through the contact form. We will ask for anything we need through an appropriate channel.

How we use it, and our lawful basis

PurposeLawful basis (UK GDPR)
Replying to your enquiry and arranging a consultationLegitimate interests in responding to people who contact us; or steps before entering a contract
Delivering and managing an engagementPerformance of a contract
Keeping accounting and tax recordsLegal obligation
Keeping the website secure and workingLegitimate interests in operating a secure website

We do not sell your data, use it for automated decision-making, or add you to a mailing list. If we ever want to send you marketing, we will ask first.

Who we share it with

We use GitHub Pages (GitHub) to host this website and Formspree to receive, process and store contact-form enquiries and to notify us of new submissions. Our email provider also processes correspondence and notifications. We may share personal data with professional advisers or where the law requires us to. We do not publish submitted enquiries on the website.

International transfers

Some of our providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement: up to 24 months after our last contact.
  • Engagement and financial records: six years after the engagement ends, to meet legal and tax obligations.
  • Server logs: in line with our hosting provider’s standard retention.

Keeping it secure

We use reputable providers, encrypted connections, multi-factor authentication and access limited to the people who need it.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive a copy in a portable format. Email david.higgins@mindmachine.io and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Our web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address so it can deliver them. If we add analytics in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this notice.

Changes

We will update this notice when our practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
