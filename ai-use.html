How we use AI

Last updated 17 November 2026

AI is central to how we work. This page sets out how we use it, how we protect your data and code, and where responsibility sits.

Our principles

  • People are accountable. A named, experienced engineer reviews and approves AI-assisted work before it is accepted. AI does not make final decisions on your behalf.
  • Your tools, your models. We work with the AI tools and models you approve. We do not introduce new tools into your environment without your agreement.
  • Your data stays yours. We do not use your code, documents or data to train AI models, and we only use services configured so that inputs are not used for training.
  • You own what we build. Code and other deliverables created for you, with or without AI assistance, are yours under the terms of our agreement.
  • Transparency. We tell you where and how AI is used in your engagement, and we keep a record of approvals at each stage of delivery.

How we protect quality

AI-generated code goes through the same controls as code written by hand: versioned specifications, automated tests, independent review and named approval gates. Production access and deployments stay with people.

Confidentiality and security

Before an engagement starts we agree which AI services may be used, where data is processed and what may be shared with them. Where your policies require it, we work entirely within your environment and accounts.

Regulation

We follow the UK Government’s principles for responsible AI and keep track of emerging regulation, including the EU AI Act where it applies to our clients. We will help you meet your own obligations where our work touches them.

Our own use of AI

We also use AI tools in running our business, for example to help draft documents and this website. A person reviews everything before it is published or sent.

Questions

Email info@mindmachine.io. We review this statement as our practices and the regulatory landscape change.
