Founder

David Higgins

David first studied artificial intelligence in the late 1990s, while reading Theology at Cambridge. It was an unusual place to meet computer science. Now that AI has moved to the centre of how software is built, David has returned to those early questions: not only what AI can do, but how to use it with rigour and structure.

Over twenty-five years David has led commercial organisations, delivered technology products that people use every day, and worked alongside exceptional engineers. MindMachine brings those threads together. It starts from first principles, free of legacy.

MindMachine uses AI where it earns its place. Judgement, relationships and creativity stay with its people. The aim is simple to state and hard to execute: engineering partnerships that are faster, higher quality and better value, and that a CTO can trust.

Why “MindMachine”

MindMachine takes its name from God and the Mind Machine, John Puddefoot’s 1996 book on computers, artificial intelligence and the human soul. David read it at Cambridge, where he began studying AI and machine learning as part of his Theology degree. The question it asked has stayed with him through 25 years in enterprise technology: as machines do more, what should stay with us?

MindMachine is his answer. Use the machine for volume and speed. Keep the mind for judgement and sign-off.
